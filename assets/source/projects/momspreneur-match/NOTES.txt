Momspreneur Match — execution platform
A mission-based platform that turns business goals into tracked workflows, with collaboration pods for accountability and a real-time dashboard across the member base.

The problem
Members had goals but no structure for executing against them.
No accountability loop, so momentum died after the first week.
No visibility into who was progressing and who had stalled.
What we built
Mission engine converting goals into tracked, sequenced work
AI assistant for guidance and content refinement
Collaboration pods for peer accountability
Real-time progress and engagement dashboard
Stack
React · Node · AI assistant
